# Question bank — `working-philosophy`

> **This file is `references/questions.md` in the `working-philosophy` skill's folder**, read
> whole before the first question.
>
> - It is written in English and said in the owner's language, the one *How it speaks* names in
>   `RESEARCHER.md`: the opening, every *Ask*, *Then*, *Plain*, *Example* and *Teaches* line, every
>   *Short* label, option labels and the retake's lines. Words for the owner never mark their
>   gender: in Spanish, *me dedico a*, never *soy abogado* or *soy abogada*.
> - IDs are stable and never renumbered; a retake compares by ID. W is a question, O4 the rules,
>   C2 the closing. IDs stay in English in every file, with the other labels the skills parse:
>   *Work*, *Round*, *(round N)*, *kept*, *changed*, *new*, *still open*, and the statuses *not
>   sure yet*, *skipped* and *explained*.
> - The questions tell one story, in order: how the owner got here, what changed them, what they
>   believe now, how they decide, and what would change their mind. Each is short and open: one
>   thing to answer, never a list to fill in.
> - No answer is right or wrong. Nothing is scored. No line names a product, proposes what to do
>   or judges an answer.
> - Only what the owner types reaches `Philosophy/HOW-I-WORK.md`, each answer behind its *Short*
>   question. A pick, an example and every other line of this file stay out of it. O4's rules go
>   under *Non-negotiables* in `RESEARCHER.md`; a work typed in C2 goes on *Find first*.

## How a card reads

- **ID.** It stays the same across rounds, and a retake compares answers by it.
- **Short.** The question in at most eight words, in the owner's language. It is written in bold
  in front of the owner's answer in `HOW-I-WORK.md`, so the line still makes sense a year later.
  It is a label, never the owner's view.
- **Why.** One plain sentence said before the *Ask*: why the question is asked. Never written.
- **Ask.** The question: one thing to answer, worded as a conversation, not a form.
- **Then.** A follow-up, asked in a message of its own once the *Ask* has a typed answer — never
  after *not sure yet*, *skip* or *explain*'s third step. Its typed words join the *Ask*'s as a
  further line of the same answer, under the same *Short* and ID.
- **Plain.** Said on *explain*: one plain line, then the question again in its plainest form.
- **Example.** Said on *example*: two or three answers other people give, labelled as examples,
  never written. An owner who says one again in their own words has typed it.
- **Teaches.** One idea, said after any answer, *not sure yet* and *skip* included. Never a verdict
  on the answer.
- **Lands under.** The heading of `HOW-I-WORK.md` where a typed answer goes.

## How every question is asked

- **Open questions are asked in chat**, one per message. Each opens with where the round stands —
  *question 2 of 5 · about 8 minutes left* — then its *Why*, then the *Ask*, and ends with:
  > *Answer in your words — a story is welcome, one line is enough. Or say: example · explain ·
  > not sure yet · skip.*
- **Pick questions** (O4, C2) use the question tool (`AskUserQuestion` in Claude Code), at most
  four options plus *Other*. Without a question tool (Codex), one chat message with the options
  numbered and *Other — your own words* last.
- **The escapes** are recognised in the owner's language and their own wording:

| The owner says | In Spanish, for instance | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *explain* | *explica* | Say *Plain*, then the question in its plainest form. |
| *explain* again | *explica* | Show the *Example*. |
| *explain* a third time | *explica* | Recorded as *explained*; asked again next round. |
| *example* | *ejemplo* | Show the *Example*. |
| *not sure yet*, *I don't know* | *no sé todavía*, *no sé* | A complete answer. Nothing is written to `HOW-I-WORK.md`; the round file records it. |
| *skip* | *salta* | Recorded as *skipped*, and asked first next round. |
| *stop* | *para* | Straight to the preview with what is already answered. |
| *keep*, on a retake | *igual*, *mantener* | The earlier answer stands; nothing is added. |
| *my rules*, on a retake | *mis reglas* | Once the current question is answered, ask O4. |

## Before the first question (said, not asked)

> No test, and no right answers. Five short questions about how you work — how you got here, what
> changed you, what you believe, how you decide and what would change your mind — then the rules
> you want me to keep. About ten minutes. Say *skip* or *stop* any time; *not sure yet* is a
> complete answer.
>
> Only what you type goes into `Philosophy/HOW-I-WORK.md`, word for word, each answer behind its
> question, and the rules into `RESEARCHER.md` — after you've seen them and said go.

Then `Ready?` (`¿Comenzamos?`), with *Start* or *Not now*. On *Not now*, nothing is written; say
how to start later. On a retake, the opening of *Taking it again* instead.

## The questions, in the order asked

### W1 · How you got here
- *Lands under:* **Why I do this work**.
- **Short:** *How I got here* (*Cómo llegué aquí*).
- **Why:** Where you come from explains what doing your work well means to you.
- **Ask:** How did you get to what you do today? Tell it as a story — where it started, and why
  you stayed.
- **Plain:** Not a CV: the reason behind the path.
- **Example:**
  - "I studied law to fix one unfair thing in my town, and found I liked the arguing."
  - "I fell into finance by accident and stayed because markets never stop asking questions."
- **Teaches:** Why you do it decides what *doing it well* means to you — the yardstick the rest of
  your answers lean on.

### W3 · What changed you
- *Lands under:* **What I have learned**.
- **Short:** *What changed how I work* (*Qué cambió mi forma de trabajar*).
- **Why:** A lesson tied to a moment is one memory can't quietly rewrite.
- **Ask:** Was there a moment — a win, a mistake, a person — that changed how you work? What
  happened?
- **Then:** What do you do differently since?
- **Plain:** One moment is enough; a mistake you won't repeat counts.
- **Example:**
  - "I lost a case by trusting my first read. Now I read the whole file before forming a view."
  - "A boss asked me why five times. I still do it."
- **Teaches:** A lesson is evidence you already own. Written down, it stops being a story you tell
  and becomes a rule you can keep.

### W2 · What you believe
- *Lands under:* **What I believe about my field**.
- **Short:** *What I believe that others don't* (*Lo que creo y otros no*).
- **Why:** A belief written down can be checked; one kept in your head only gets confirmed.
- **Ask:** What do you believe about your work that many people in it wouldn't agree with?
- **Plain:** A hunch counts, and it doesn't have to be right.
- **Example:**
  - "Most disputes are settled before anyone reaches a courtroom."
  - "Experience beats credentials."
- **Teaches:** A belief written down can be checked against what you read and what happens.

### W4 · How you decide
- *Lands under:* **How I decide**.
- **Short:** *How I decide something important* (*Cómo decido algo importante*).
- **Why:** A way of deciding you can name is one you can improve.
- **Ask:** Think of the last hard call you made at work. How did you decide?
- **Then:** Is that how you usually decide, or was that one different?
- **Plain:** What did you need to see, whom did you ask, when did you stop looking?
- **Example:**
  - "I write the best argument against my position first."
  - "I sleep on anything I can't explain in two sentences."
- **Teaches:** A way of deciding you can name is one you can improve; one you can't name repeats
  its mistakes quietly.

### W5 · What would change your mind
- *Lands under:* **What would change my mind**.
- **Short:** *What would change my mind* (*Qué me haría cambiar de opinión*).
- **Why:** A belief you would drop on evidence is one you can test.
- **Ask:** You said "<the W2 line, quoted>". What would you need to see to stop believing it?
  With no W2 line, ask about any belief on their work they'd like to test.
- **Plain:** If you were wrong about it, how would you find out?
- **Example:**
  - "If the cases I settle early turned out worse for my clients."
  - "If newcomers with other training kept doing better than me."
- **Teaches:** Naming what would end a belief turns it from an opinion into something you can
  test. This is the line I'll quote first when you take this again.

### O4 · Your rules
- *Asked:* round 1, after W5, as a multi-select tool question. On a retake, after W5 while
  *Non-negotiables* holds no rule, and otherwise only when the owner says *my rules*. Never in a
  home whose `AGENTS.md` names no skill among the writers of *Non-negotiables*, as the skill's
  *step 1* says.
- *Lands under:* never `Philosophy/`, and no line in the round file. On the round's go, the rules
  it settles go under *Non-negotiables* in `RESEARCHER.md`; *skip* or *stop* leaves that section
  as it is.
- Say first: *Last thing: a few rules, decided on a calm day — the ones I'll keep to with you, in
  everything we do.*
- Header: `Your rules` (`Tus reglas`).
- **Ask:** Which rules should I keep to with you? The question shows two, a plain line each, as
  they will be written, but for *my*, which the file gives as the researcher's name:
  - *Every result comes from a tested tool, never from my head.*
  - *An idea is written down before it is tested, and every claim names its source — one without
    a source is marked as a guess.*
- **Options:**

| Option | Description shown |
| --- | --- |
| *Keep these two* (recommended) | Both, as written above. |
| *Add one of my own* | A mistake you never want to repeat — one line, in chat, after. |
| *Change or drop one* | Which one, and how — one line, in chat, after. |
| *Add the two for strategies* | For when you test an investment strategy: *An idea gets real money only after a test written down beforehand, never after one good month.* *Every plan is challenged before it is tested, and every idea tried is counted, the failed ones too.* |

- **The two for strategies.** When *Here for* holds *Build and test a strategy*, they are shown
  with the two from the start, four lines, and *Keep these four* (recommended) takes the place of
  *Keep these two* and of *Add the two for strategies*. Otherwise *Add the two for strategies* is
  an option only when the owner asks for them, in any words.
- **What is kept.** Whatever is not changed or dropped is kept. A rule typed under *Other* is a
  rule of the owner's own. After *Keep these two* or *Keep these four* alone nothing more is asked.
- **A rule about holdings** — how much in one place, when to sell, when to look again — is not
  written under *Non-negotiables*: say in one line that it belongs in `Portfolio/RULES.md`, the
  file `brief` quotes, which `brief setup` starts and the owner writes. This skill never writes in
  `Portfolio/`.
- **A home with rules already** shows them first, word for word, and they stay unless *Change or
  drop one* names one. When they hold every rule shown in any words, *Keep my rules (current)*
  takes the place of *Keep these two* or *Keep these four*; when they hold some, *Keep my rules
  (current)* comes first and *Keep mine, add the rest* names each rule it would add. A rule held
  in any words is never added again.
- **Plain words only.** Every rule is written as plainly as here, in the owner's language.

### C2 · What to read next
- *Asked:* last, as a single-select tool question. Header `Read next` (`Aprender`).
- *Lands under:* never `Philosophy/`. A work typed under *Other* goes, on the go, on the closing
  *Find first* line of `RESEARCHER.md`, in the owner's words, unless the preview leaves that line
  alone.
- **Options:**
  - up to two files *in your Sources/, not yet read*, which only need `read` and go on no *Find
    first* line — the hand-over names the one picked by its file name;
  - `teach <a topic the owner's notes cover>` when `Knowledge/` holds a note, otherwise *Learn one
    idea with me — after your first reading*;
  - *Not now*;
  - and *Other* for a work of the owner's own.

Then the preview.

## Where each answer lands

A `HOW-I-WORK.md` that `Philosophy/` lacks is created on the round's go with
`# How I work — <the owner's work, in their words>` and these five headings, in this order, in the
owner's language, and nothing else.

| Heading in `HOW-I-WORK.md` | IDs |
| --- | --- |
| **Why I do this work** | W1 |
| **What I believe about my field** | W2 |
| **What I have learned** | W3 |
| **How I decide** | W4 |
| **What would change my mind** | W5 |
| *never written to `HOW-I-WORK.md`* | O4 (its rules go under *Non-negotiables*, but one about holdings), C2, *not sure yet*, *skipped*, *explained* |

Each typed answer is one bullet: `- **<Short>** <their words> *(round N)*`, the *Short* in the
owner's language; an answer of several lines, a *Then*'s included, keeps them, indented under its
bullet. Lines an earlier round wrote with no *Short* in front stay as they are.

The round file's first line has `Work` where an investing round has its level:
`# Round <N> · <YYYY-MM-DD> · Work`. A round file an earlier version wrote with `Field` there is a
round of this skill too.

## Taking it again

### The opening, instead of the first one

> Round <N> was on <date>; you answered <a> of <b>. Since then you've read <notes, each linked>.
>
> You said this would change your mind, in round <its round>: "<W5's latest typed answer, word for
> word>".
>
> Your rules in `RESEARCHER.md` stay as they are; say *my rules* to change them.

- *a* counts the questions with words, kept ones included; *b* the questions the round asked.
- If nothing was read, say so plainly, with no nudge. The W5 line is said only when a round holds
  typed words for it.

### The scope question

`Retake?` (`¿Repetir?`):
- *The whole round* — W1 to W5, in the order above, then O4 as its card says, then C2.
- *Only what I left open* — suggested when a question's latest status is *not sure yet*,
  *skipped* or *explained*, or no round has a line for it; left out when none is.
- *Just show me what changed* — no questions, and nothing written.

### Each question asked again

- **Answered before:** quote the earlier answer, word for word, and its round, then ask:
  > *Still true? Type* keep*, or say it again in new words.*
- **Left open:** asked first, marked *(open since round N)*.
- A question an earlier round answered under an ID this bank no longer asks is shown in the
  comparison as *no longer asked*, its answer standing in `HOW-I-WORK.md`.

### The comparison

Shown in chat before the preview, never written to a file: one line of counts (kept, changed, new,
still open) and the notes read in between, then a table — ID · *Short* · then (round, date) · now ·
label. A standing line in `HOW-I-WORK.md` that today's answer cuts against is quoted, with *edit
it by hand, or run `refine`, if it no longer holds*.
