# Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen (2017) — *A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing*

## What it says

Time-series momentum delivered positive average returns in every decade since 1880, with low
correlation to traditional assets, and performed well in eight of the ten largest crisis periods —
the property the authors call "crisis alpha".
