# Asness, Moskowitz & Pedersen (2013) — *Value and Momentum Everywhere*

## What it says

Value and momentum produce return premia across eight markets and asset classes, with momentum
positively correlated to momentum elsewhere and negatively correlated to value — pointing to common
global factors rather than asset-specific stories.
