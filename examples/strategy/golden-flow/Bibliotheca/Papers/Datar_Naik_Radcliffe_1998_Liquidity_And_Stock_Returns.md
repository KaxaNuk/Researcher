# Datar, Naik & Radcliffe (1998) — *Liquidity and Stock Returns: An Alternative Test*

## What it says

Uses turnover as the liquidity proxy and finds returns decrease with turnover — the same direction
as Amihud, through a measure much closer to the dollar-volume rank Golden Flow actually uses.

## What it implies for Golden Flow

> Rewritten on 2026-10-27 for this copy, after FINDINGS_1.md reported on Sharadar.
>
> **Claim 2, the sizing: the closest analogue, pointing the other way.** Turnover is the published
> measure nearest to Golden Flow's traded value, and the paper finds it predicts lower returns.
>
> Among the fifty most traded members, the analyzer splits by period. The traded-value rank's
> information coefficient over a year is +0.0130 over 2000–2026, with the expected sign on 51.0% of
> dates: −0.0504 in 2000–2008 and +0.0825 in 2018–2026 ([`RESULTS.md`](../../RESULTS.md), *Before
> any experiment*, row 8). The book follows the same split: it lost to the index in 2000–2008 and
> beat it in 2018–2026. A sign that turns with the period is not settled by one window, so the paper
> keeps its place against claim 2.
