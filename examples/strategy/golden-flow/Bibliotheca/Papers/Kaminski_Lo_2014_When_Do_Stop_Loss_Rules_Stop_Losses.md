# Kaminski & Lo (2014) — *When Do Stop-Loss Rules Stop Losses?*

## What it says

Stop-loss rules add value precisely when returns have positive serial correlation — momentum regimes
— and destroy value under mean reversion. Empirically, volatility-scaled stop rules on equity
futures raised monthly returns by roughly 1.5% while cutting volatility substantially.
