# Daniel & Moskowitz (2016) — *Momentum Crashes*

## What it says

Momentum crashes are not random. They cluster in post-panic rebounds, when the *short* side of a
momentum book — the beaten-down names — rips upward while volatility is falling from its peak. A
dynamic strategy scaling by forecast mean and variance beats constant-volatility scaling.
